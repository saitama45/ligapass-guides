# LigaPass guides

Step-by-step guides with pictures for **LigaPass**, the Android app for running basketball leagues.

Every guide uses the app's built-in **demo**: a made-up league you can open on your phone without an account, as any of its people. In the pictures, a **yellow ring** marks what to tap next.

## Start here

| Guide | What it covers |
|---|---|
| [Getting started](getting-started/README.md) | Getting the app, signing in, player sign-up, the demo, finding your way around, and your profile. |

## Guides for each person

| Guide | For | What it covers |
|---|---|---|
| [League owner](league-owner/README.md) | The person who owns the organization and its leagues | Buying season credits, giving your staff accounts, starting a season, sponsors and the live-stream look, the league office (fees, attendance, the league's listing, Game of the Week), and insights and sharing. |
| [Commissioner](commissioner/README.md) | The person who runs a season day to day | Teams and rosters, the schedule, confirming results, standings, stats and season awards. |
| [Scorer](scorer/README.md) | The person who keeps score at the court | A whole game: check-in, the starting five, the scorer console and every kind of play, the clock, substitutions and timeouts, fixing mistakes, and handing in the result. |
| [Team manager](team-manager/README.md) | A team's manager | Your team's roster, adding players for approval, the team logo, who leads the team, attendance, and your team's games and standings. |
| [Player](player/README.md) | A player | Your next game, your schedule across leagues, your stats, game pages and box scores, finding a league and following a team. |

## Features

| Guide | What it covers |
|---|---|
| [Go live with no stream key](go-live-connect/README.md) | **New in 1.0.0 (20).** Connect Facebook or YouTube once, then go live on your Page or channel with one tap: no stream key, no Live Producer. |
| [One phone scores, another streams](camera-phone/README.md) | **New in 1.0.0 (19).** A camera phone on a tripod streams while the scorer scores at the table: pair them with a code, call breaks from the table, and show your league's logo, banner and break picture, with countdowns at timeouts and between periods. |
| [Live streaming](live-stream-demo/README.md) | Stream a game to Facebook (or YouTube) from the scorer's console with a stream key, with the scoreboard drawn over the camera. |
| [Suspensions, bans and fines](discipline/README.md) | Suspend or ban a player, fine a player or a team, see who sits out a game, and keep track of every fine — with a purse for the season's MVP. |
